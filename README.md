## Research

**PathBridger** · 2026 · *Under Review*  
Hierarchical offline goal-conditioned RL. The method proposes an endpoint, draws an explicit bridge through state space, and decodes that short stretch into actions.

[Code](https://github.com/SChoish/PathBridger)

**Policy optimization** · ongoing  
I am also working on **multi-step proximal policy improvement** (re-centered refinement instead of a single update) and **adaptive policy optimization** (step size, depth, and interpolation between a behavior-supported policy and a multi-step teacher).

[MPI](https://github.com/seonvin0319/MPI) · [POGO](https://github.com/seonvin0319/POGO)

Next I want to take PathBridger further: offline-to-online learning, action-free decision making, and longer-horizon generative control.
